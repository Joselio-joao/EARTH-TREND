METHOD WORKBENCH

What a trend report should include

↗

Direction

Positive, negative or no clear direction, based on the selected estimator.

∆

Magnitude

Express change in meaningful units and per time unit where appropriate.

±

Uncertainty

Report confidence intervals or other uncertainty measures when justified.

SCIENTIFIC CHECKLIST

Before interpreting results

SeasonalityCheck whether recurring seasonal cycles influence the trend estimate.
SignificanceUse an appropriate test and state assumptions; statistical significance is not practical importance.
Data qualityInspect gaps, quality flags, changes in sensors and temporal consistency.
LimitationsDistinguish observation, statistical association and causal explanation.
