ANALYSIS PIPELINE

Six steps for a defensible result

01

Frame the question

Specify region, variable, period and scientific motivation.

02

Select data

Use a product whose resolution, units and coverage match the question.

03

Prepare

Handle missing values, quality flags and aggregation transparently.

04

Estimate trend

Choose a suitable method and test its assumptions.

05

Quantify uncertainty

Report uncertainty, significance and limitations where supported.

06

Communicate

Separate data evidence from interpretation and possible explanations.
