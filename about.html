NASA SPACE APPS CHALLENGE

Be an Earth System Trend Detective.

EarthTrend AI is a concept for helping students and curious citizens explore environmental trends and learn how to interpret Earth science data.

OUR PURPOSE

Make Earth data easier to investigate.

The project aims to connect a clear research question, accessible datasets, transparent analysis and understandable visual communication.

This frontend is a prototype. It is not an official NASA product and does not imply NASA endorsement.
